SKILLED MIGRANTS IN THE NETHERLANDS

Highly skilled migrants come to the Netherlands to work in jobs that require specific knowledge and expertise.

They are often actively recruited by Dutch companies and institutions.

SHARP DECLINES IN THE NUMBER OF SKILLED MIGRANTS AFTER 2022

In 2024, 16.000 skilled migrants from outside the EU came to the Netherlands.

This was 26% fewer than in 2023.

decline reflected in numbers

After years of growth, the number of highly skilled immigrants is now actually declining. That is notable.

Why is this decline notable?

Skilled migrants play an important role in sectors where expertise is in short supply, such as the IT sector.

A decline in the number of highly skilled immigrants could have a significant impact on innovation and economic development.

Work

They don’t come here without reason

Highly skilled immigrants do not come to the Netherlands by chance. They are primarily attracted to specific positions and sectors where their expertise is needed.

These are often jobs that are difficult to fill

Industry & manufacturing

22% of the skilled migrants work in this sector. They improve and monitor complex production processes such as those involving machinery, medical technology, and precision components thereby contributing to quality and efficiency.

Distribution & logistics

18% of the skilled immigrants, many work in warehouses and distribution centers. There, highly skilled immigrants keep the systems for transporting goods running smoothly.

ict

9% of the skilled migrants, many work in the IT sector. They develop software, apps, and digital systems that are used in everyday life.

Origin

Where do skilled migrants come from?

Skilled migrants come mainly from non-EU countries.

They are brought to the Netherlands to work in jobs that require specialized knowledge.

1. United States

13% are from the United States. They often work in technology and research.

2. Turkey

6% 13% are from Turkey. They often work in various knowledge-intensive sectors.

3. India

29% of skilled migrants from outside the EU/EFTA come from India. They are primarily active in ICT and technology.

4. China

10% of highly skilled immigrants come from China. They are primarily active in industry and science.

5. Japan

5% are from Japan. They often work in engineering and industry.
