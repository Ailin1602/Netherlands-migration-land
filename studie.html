WELCOME TO STUDY MIGRATION

Student migrants come to the Netherlands to study at an educational institution recognized by the Dutch government, such as a high school, mbo, hbo, or university. They enroll in full-time programs, thereby contributing to the advancement of knowledge and international cooperation.

Arrival

Studying in the Netherlands!

Every year, many students from abroad choose to study in the Netherlands.

In the 2022–2023 academic year, that number was nearly

123 000

international students enrolled at Dutch universities or hbo's

International students come from a variety of countries, both within and outside Europe. The largest groups are often from European countries, but students from Asia, Africa, and North and South America are also increasingly choosing to study in the Netherlands.

International students in the Netherlands tend to choose programs with a strong international focus.

Below are the most popular fields of study in the Netherlands

Economics & Business Administration

The most popular degree program focusing on international trade and entrepreneurship.

Social & Behavioral Studies

Research on society and human behavior in a global context.

Technology & IT

Strong international presence with a focus on innovation and digitalization.

Law & Governance

Study programs with an international and European scope.

Natural Sciences & Research

Research-based education with international collaboration.

These choices of study programs show that the Netherlands is primarily viewed as a knowledge-based country with strong academic programs.

Housing

Where do international students live?

International students often live in the city where they study, but finding a place to live can be a challenge.

60% lives in privately rented housing

25% lives in student housing

15% lives with family or other options

Major student cities

Most international students do not study all over the Netherlands; instead, they are mainly found in major student cities.

1. Groningen

belangrijke studentenstad met een groot aandeel uitwonende studenten en bekend om sterke internationale aantrekkingskracht

2. Amsterdam

a major student city with a large proportion of students living away from home and known for its strong international appeal

4. Utrecht

A centrally located student city with a large university and college, home to many international students.

5. Maastricht

the municipality with the highest concentration of international students per resident, particularly near the university.

Housing challenges

International students often have trouble finding affordable and available housing.

In major student cities, many international students live in private rental housing. Due to the high number of students and limited supply, it is difficult to find affordable housing, which increases pressure on the rental market.
